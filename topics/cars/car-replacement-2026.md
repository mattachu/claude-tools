### Battery generation — resolved by inference, not yet by BMW dealer confirmation
The seller initially believed this is a **Generation 5 (19.5kWh)** battery with an 8-year/100,000-mile warranty. Three independent pieces of evidence instead point to **Generation 4 (12kWh)**:
1. The original Autotrader listing itself states "330e **12kWh**"
2. BMW's own press material dates the Gen 5/19.5kWh update to **May 2024** — this car was registered **19 March 2024**, before that update existed
3. The real-world ~30-mile EV range matches the 12kWh pack's WLTP rating (up to 37mi) far better than the 19.5kWh pack's (up to 61–63mi)

**This should still be confirmed definitively** by a BMW dealer looking up the VIN, per BMW's own guidance — this determines which warranty term actually applies (see below).

## Warranty position
- **Manufacturer 3-year warranty**: ends **19 March 2027** (~6 months left as of Sept 2026)
- **HV battery warranty**: if Gen 4 as the evidence suggests, **6 years / 62,000 miles** from registration (ends March 2030 / at 62,000 miles) — at 41,000 miles now, that's **~21,000 miles headroom, roughly 2 years** at Dad's usage rate. If it somehow turns out to be Gen 5, the term would instead be 8 years/100,000 miles with much more headroom.
- BMW's PHEV battery warranty **transfers to new owners**, conditional on the on-board service-indicator intervals having been kept up
- No dealer warranty on this purchase (private sale) — only the manufacturer/battery warranty above, plus whatever protections a private sale carries in law
